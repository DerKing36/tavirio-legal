Deleting Wortavia data

This page explains how users of the Wortavia Android app from Tavirio Games can delete data or request its deletion.

No account: Wortavia does not use its own user accounts. Normal game data is stored locally on the device.

1. Delete local game data yourself

Local data includes progress, coins, completed levels, statistics, streaks, settings and unfinished puzzles. You can delete it through Android Settings by clearing Wortavia's app data/storage, or by uninstalling the app. Tavirio Games does not keep this normal data in its own cloud and therefore generally cannot delete it remotely.

The optional closed-test save export/import feature creates or reads a file at a location you choose through Android. Clearing app data or uninstalling does not necessarily delete any separately exported file; delete that file from its storage location if you no longer want it. Importing a save does not create or alter a Google Play purchase entitlement.

2. Request deletion of data held by Tavirio Games

If you sent personal data directly to Tavirio Games, for example in a support email, you can request deletion. Email taviriogames@gmail.com with the subject “Wortavia data deletion”. Briefly identify the data or earlier correspondence. We may need further information to locate the correspondence. Tavirio Games will delete data under its responsibility unless a legal retention duty or another lawful reason prevents immediate deletion. Requests are handled without undue delay and within applicable legal deadlines.

3. Data that may be deleted

Support emails and other personal content sent directly to Tavirio Games, and any other personal data it holds and can identify as yours, may be deleted subject to legal retention duties. You delete local game data yourself as described above.

4. Data that may need to be retained

Data subject to statutory retention requirements or needed to establish, exercise or defend legal claims may remain until the applicable retention or limitation period ends, and is then deleted unless another legal basis applies.

5. Google services

Wortavia uses or integrates Google services including Google Mobile Ads/AdMob, Google Play Billing and Google Play In-App Review. Tavirio Games cannot directly erase data that Google processes under its own responsibility from Google accounts or systems. Use Google's privacy and account controls; see Google's Privacy Policy.
