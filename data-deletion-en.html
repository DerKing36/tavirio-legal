Deleting Wortavia data

This page explains Wortavia's separate deletion routes. Deleting local data does not erase server records or cancel a Google Play purchase. Where possible, complete desired server deletion online and check confirmation before removing local credentials.

1. Local saves and exported backups

Progress, coins, levels, daily puzzles, missions, badges, streaks and settings are normally stored locally. You can remove them through Android's app settings by clearing app data or uninstalling the app. This can irretrievably erase progress; Tavirio Games provides no general cloud copy of your save.

Separately exported backups remain at the location you selected and must be deleted there. A copy at a cloud provider you selected is not erased by uninstalling the app either. Importing a save does not create or change a Google Play purchase entitlement.

2. Optional gameplay statistics

Our analysis remains off without enablement, valid specific consent and sufficient individual eligibility. If enabled for you, you can withdraw through Settings → “Ads and consent settings” or the privacy options. New collection and uploads stop and unsent optional events are discarded. The complete game remains available.

Protected deletion authorization is retained for the outstanding server request. Offline it is stored locally, and transmitted or checked only during later active online use. An authorized request reaching the server blocks the installation and initiates removal of the active association, events, batches, coin snapshots and participation records. Failures are retried. Local withdrawal or a successful transmission attempt is not confirmed server deletion; there is no general 24-hour guarantee.

Regular analytics cleanup runs every 15 minutes and removes raw events, batches and snapshots received 89 days and 23 hours ago. Limited consent, registration and replay evidence may normally remain for up to 90 days after creation of the respective record; confirmed deletion status for seven days. Failures or service pauses can delay processing. Unsent local events have a seven-day upload window and are cleaned on the next access. Closed apps run no dedicated background deletion process. Retaining long-term aggregates for 13 months has not been approved.

3. Support tickets, images and local conversation copies

Public in-app support submission has not yet been enabled. Where ticket access is available, “Delete request” removes the ticket, messages, replies, optional image and read markers after successful server processing. A content-free hashed deletion marker may remain for seven days to handle retries safely. Support is independent of analytics; analytics withdrawal does not erase a conversation.

Support tickets become due for deletion 90 days after the last message or status change and no more than 180 days after ticket creation. Viewing alone does not extend this period. Automatic support cleanup normally runs every 15 minutes and removes due tickets and their messages, images and status data; expired deletion markers are also cleaned up. Failures or service pauses can delay processing. Deletion requested from the device remains pending offline or after errors until confirmed by the server. Locally encrypted text and ticket credentials are separate; the conversation cache has no automatic time-based expiry. Removing a local copy is not confirmed server deletion.

Anonymous association cannot be guaranteed after data loss or uninstallation without the retained secret ticket credential. Your legal rights remain; we assess proportionate alternative identification. Do not email secret ticket credentials or tokens.

4. Purchase and entitlement evidence

Server-side purchase records have no automatic deletion button or general time-based deletion. Active evidence supports lifetime access and restoration of “Remove ads permanently”; revocation records prevent invalid purchases from being reactivated. These include a token hash, package/product identifiers, entitlement/verification status, timestamps and refund/revocation information. Full purchase tokens and order identifiers are not persisted in the purchase database.

You can request deletion. We assess which minimal evidence remains necessary to provide the purchased service, prevent abuse lawfully or meet legal obligations. Erasing evidence can make later verification or restoration harder; it does not automatically cancel a Google Play purchase and is not itself proof of a refund. Analytics withdrawal or support deletion does not revoke purchased entitlements.

5. Hosting logs, safety copies and Google

HTTPS services may process connection and technical operational data, including IP address, approximate region derived from it, request time and response status. Under the Supabase Free plan, API and database logs are normally accessible for one day. This is not a guaranteed physical deletion deadline for all provider logs or safety copies. Customer-accessible automatic daily database backups are not included in Free; this does not mean there are no internal provider copies. Clearing app data does not erase them.

Provider logs and necessary copies follow applicable contractual operational, security and legal requirements; specific rights requests are handled with the provider. Tavirio Games cannot directly erase data that Google processes independently for AdMob, UMP, Play Billing, Play Integrity or other Google services from Google accounts. Use Google's privacy and account controls as well: Google's Privacy Policy.

6. Request deletion and exercise other rights

Email taviriogames@gmail.com with the subject “Wortavia data deletion”. Describe the processing or request concerned; for purchases you can provide the available Google Play order identifier. Do not send passwords, full purchase tokens, payment-card details or secret access credentials. Further proportionate information may be needed for safe identification.

Requests are assessed without undue delay within applicable legal deadlines. Data is erased unless necessary retention or another lawful reason prevents it. Subject to the legal requirements, you have rights including access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection, withdrawal of consent for the future and complaint to a data protection authority. Support emails follow the handling period and any legal obligations, not an automatically operating ticket deadline.

8. Replacement rewards and claim records

The replacement reward service is in place. Reward grants are currently disabled. Protected data access is technically available and has been tested with the Google Play test version. Erasure is performed only on an explicit request and is considered complete only after a confirmed server response. Automatic cleanup of the private ledger runs every 15 minutes under normal operation.

A verification challenge is valid for two minutes. A technical marker that prevents proof reuse expires after ten minutes. Quota counters expire after two of their respective time windows. Closure markers become due for deletion after seven days, confirmed daily puzzle records after 30 days and confirmed hint records after 24 hours. Minimal replay-prevention markers for previously granted hints may remain until the programme ends. Campaign records become due for deletion at least 30 days after the grant and only after the fixed programme end. The next successful cleanup removes records due for deletion. Separate terms for provider logs and backups are described in section 5.

If enabled later, the programme end fixed at setup is at most 30 days away. New grants are disabled after that date. If the service is reachable, pending claims can be reconciled for up to 30 days after the programme ends. An available deletion endpoint can still be used when only reward grants are disabled.

In Wortavia 1.3.0 (from build 20), you can explicitly confirm an erasure request under Settings → Legal → Privacy → Delete reward data. From build 21, the protected “Access my data” option is also provided there. The request needs a connection to the available service, the existing private installation credential and fresh Play Integrity proof tied to erasure. The app treats the request as erased only after a confirmed server response. If you are offline, the endpoint is disabled or the private credential is missing, the app cannot confirm server erasure. If a request remains unconfirmed, you can explicitly retry it. The app does not resend it automatically.

After confirmed erasure, the server removes the claim records and quota counters linked to the credential. Unexpired technical verification records that are not directly linked to that credential in the ledger are cleaned up separately after expiry. While the programme is running, only a derived block record remains, with no reward, puzzle, action or time details. It prevents new claims and further credits for the same credential and becomes due for deletion when the programme ends.

After confirmed erasure, the app removes the private reward credential and retains only the erasure status needed for local display. Further replacement claims are blocked for that installation; a limited server-side blocking marker prevents further claims under the previous association until the programme ends. Regular game saves, coins already credited and purchase entitlements are kept. Reinstalling the app or clearing app data can permanently lose the previous association, after which a new private credential may be created on renewed use. Importing a save does not restore the old credential. Local removal alone does not erase server records and may make it impossible to match earlier records.

Supported app versions offer a protected “Access my data” action. For further access, erasure or objections, use the privacy contact. Describe your request without emailing private credentials, integrity tokens or game saves. We check whether the data can be safely matched using suitable and proportionate information. Without the previous credential, a match may not be possible. Withdrawing consent to optional gameplay analytics does not affect this separate ledger. See the privacy notice for replacement rewards for more information.
