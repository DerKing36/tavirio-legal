Privacy Policy for Wortavia

This policy explains how personal data may be processed when you use the Android app Wortavia.

No account: Wortavia does not require a user account. Normal game progress, coins and settings remain on your device. Separate processing concerns the Google services, purchase verification and independently enabled optional analysis and support features described below.

2. Local game data

To provide the game, Wortavia stores progress, coins, completed levels, statistics, streaks, settings and the state of an unfinished puzzle locally on your device. Tavirio Games does not store this data on its own servers and normally cannot access it. Local storage is used to provide the app features you request.

You can remove local data through Android's app-data settings or by uninstalling Wortavia. The optional save export/import feature lets you choose a file on your device or another destination through Android. Such copies are outside the app's local storage and must be deleted separately by you.

3. Google Mobile Ads / AdMob

Wortavia includes the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). For users in the European Economic Area, the provider is in particular Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland.

The Mobile Ads SDK may automatically process and transmit to Google the IP address (which may indicate an approximate location), app interactions, diagnostic data, and device or other identifiers such as an advertising ID or App Set ID. Processing may serve ad delivery and measurement, analysis, fraud prevention, security and compliance. Some rewarded ads are voluntary.

Where consent is required, it is requested through the app's consent management before the corresponding advertising processing. Consent can be changed or withdrawn for the future through the advertising and consent settings or privacy options offered in the app.

See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses data from partner apps.

4. Google User Messaging Platform (UMP)

Wortavia includes Google's User Messaging Platform to display privacy information and store or respect consent decisions. Access to or storage of information on your device for this purpose follows your choice or, where legally permitted, the technical requirements of managing that choice.

5. Google Play Billing / in-app purchases

In Wortavia version 1.3.0, you can optionally make the one-time Google Play purchase “Remove ads permanently”. It removes automatic advertising interruptions. Voluntary rewarded ads remain available. Coin packs are not offered in this version. Google Play handles payment; Tavirio Games does not receive full card or bank details.

For purchase validation, restoration and subsequent checks, the app sends its package identifier, product identifier, Google Play purchase token, purchase source and, where available, order identifier to Tavirio Games' purchase-verification service over HTTPS. This service is hosted on Supabase and checks purchase and acknowledgement status with Google Play. Confirmed refunds that remove the entitlement and revocations are also checked periodically with Google Play.

The service's database stores an internal record identifier, a SHA-256 hash of the purchase token, package and product identifiers, purchase, acknowledgement and entitlement status, verification timestamps, refund/revocation information and information about updates. The full purchase token and order identifier are processed for validation but are not persisted in this database. The full token can remain in private app storage on your device for later revalidation; it is excluded from game-save exports.

Purchase data is used to provide and restore the purchased entitlement, prevent duplicate or invalid grants and apply confirmed refunds or revocations. Normal game progress, coins, statistics and streaks are not sent to this service. No Wortavia account is required. Temporary service errors are not treated as evidence of a refund.

6. Google Play In-App Review

Wortavia may open the official Google Play in-app review feature. Using it is voluntary. Reviews and ratings you submit are processed through Google Play. In closed tests, feedback may be available privately to the developer; in production, a review may be published according to Google Play's settings.

7. Sharing and reminders

Wortavia can offer an invitation or a daily-result card through Android's share dialog. Only when you select a target app does Android pass the selected content to that app. Tavirio Games does not read your contacts or receive recipient information from this feature.

If you enable the optional daily reminder and grant notification permission, Wortavia schedules a reminder on your device. You can turn it off in the app settings or Android settings. This feature does not require an account or a Tavirio Games notification server.

8. Recipients, hosting and transfers outside the EEA

Use of the Google services above may involve transfers to Google companies and their service providers. Google operates globally. See Google's data-transfer frameworks.

Tavirio Games uses Supabase for purchase verification and the separate analytics and support services that can be used only after their respective approval, with a PostgreSQL database. Access is restricted to authorized operator or support personnel; the Tavirio Monitor provides authorized optional-statistics reports. Supabase describes its processing on behalf of customers in its Data Processing Addendum, which names Supabase Pte. Ltd. and sets out safeguards for international transfers, including standard contractual clauses where applicable. The database is hosted in eu-west-1/Ireland. This database region does not guarantee that all hosting, support or subprocessor processing is confined to the EEA.

When the HTTPS services are accessed, hosting/gateway logs may contain the IP address, approximate city/region derived from the connection, request time, route, user-agent information, response status and technical performance/diagnostic data. These records are used for service operation, troubleshooting and abuse prevention. Request limiting temporarily stores counters associated with a hashed purchase identifier and time windows. The purchase-verification code does not log full purchase tokens, order identifiers or secret credentials.

9. Retention

Local game data remains on your device until you delete it, clear app data or uninstall the app. Separately exported save files must be deleted at their storage location. Google applies its own retention rules to data processed under its responsibility. Support emails are retained as needed to handle requests and applicable legal obligations.

Server-side purchase and entitlement records are independent of local game data. There is currently no automatic time-based deletion of these records. Active records support lifetime access and restoration; revocation records prevent an invalid purchase from being activated again. Clearing local data does not erase these server records. Deletion requests are reviewed as explained below.

Old rate-limit counters are removed when a new global request is processed and the counter window is more than 15 minutes old. This does not guarantee deletion during inactivity. The Supabase project uses the Free plan. Its project-accessible API and database logs have one-day retention. Regular automatic database backups accessible to the customer are not included in this plan. This does not mean the provider processes no internal safety copies or operational logs. Such records follow the applicable contractual operational, security and legal requirements; rights requests are handled with the provider. This plan-based accessibility is not a guaranteed physical deletion deadline for all provider logs or safety copies. No general one-day deletion guarantee is made. Local app-data deletion does not delete those logs or provider backups.

We review the request and safely identify the relevant purchase. Do not send passwords, full purchase tokens or card details by email. Purchase and entitlement records are not erased through an automatic in-app deletion button; the additional analytics and support routes are described separately below and on the deletion page. According to Google, transfers made by the integrated advertising SDK are encrypted using TLS. App-to-verifier and verifier-to-Google transfers use HTTPS; the backend database connection uses certificate-checked TLS. Regular game progress remains local and no Wortavia account is required.

Purchase validation and restoration are necessary to provide the service requested with the purchase (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR). Necessary abuse prevention and technical operational diagnostics are based on legitimate interests in a secure, reliable service and preventing invalid grants (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR), unless your interests and fundamental rights override those interests. Where advertising requires consent, that consent is the basis for the corresponding processing (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR) and is managed through UMP.

14. Optional gameplay statistics

Gameplay analysis is offered only to explicitly enabled installations with valid specific consent and sufficient participation eligibility. Without these requirements it remains off. The complete game, local statistics, coins and purchased entitlements remain available if you decline or withdraw.

Data and purpose

Optional analysis helps us understand and improve Wortavia's content, difficulty, hint needs and virtual coin economy. It covers participating and active installations, new and returning participants including return after 1, 7 and 30 days, active play time, campaign starts and completions, abandoned attempts and difficulty, daily puzzles, missions and badges. German and English content is analysed separately.

It also covers authorized coin earnings from campaign, daily puzzles and missions, spending on hints, paid and free hints by level and language, and the current coin balance at most once per active day. These allow, for example, balance distributions and observed maximum, average and median values. A free hint does not prove that an advert was watched. An overall balance may include coins acquired locally from other sources; no individual purchase or advertising event is inferred from that balance.

After valid consent, a random pseudonymous installation identifier, consent epoch and unique event receipts are used. The association survives normal app restarts and prevents duplicate counting. The consent and eligibility policy version, verification time and status needed to authorize participation are also processed, without collecting a date of birth or identity documents. The data is pseudonymous, not anonymous. Reports describe only voluntarily participating installations, not all Wortavia players. Names, email addresses, target words, complete grids, saves, payment information and purchase tokens are not analytics fields. HTTPS connection records are separate.

This consent does not activate our own individual advertising-performance measurement, purchase analytics or optional error diagnostics. Challenges and boosters are not included in this feature scope. Retaining long-term aggregates for 13 months has not been approved.

Consent and participation eligibility

Specific information about our gameplay analysis is available before your choice. The existing Google consent flow covers storage or access on your device, measurement of content use and improvement of the service. General advertising consent, installation through Google Play or merely opening and closing a dialog unchanged does not replace this voluntary choice. Our own advertising-performance measurement and audience research remain excluded.

Wortavia is intended for players aged 16 and older. This store audience selection does not prove an individual player's age. Installations without legally sufficient individual eligibility are not admitted to gameplay analysis. We collect no dates of birth or identity documents for this, and add no mandatory age question or second general consent interface. Special protections for minors and advertising are considered separately.

The legal basis is voluntary consent under Article 6(1)(a) GDPR and, where consent is required for storage or access on your device, section 25(1) of the German TDDDG. Existing players are offered a new choice when additional analysis purposes are first introduced. An unchanged app version alone does not trigger another question; new purposes or materially changed processing may require renewed consent.

Limited uploads and retention

Uploads are batched and restricted to active foreground use. Regular uploads are about 30 active minutes apart; short sessions may have a limited early attempt subject to a global minimum interval. There is no dedicated analytics background process, periodic Android wakeup or permanent connection. Offline data and errors are retried only during later active use, with limited attempts and increasing delays.

The local queue is limited to 256 events or 128 KiB; each batch to 32 events or 32 KiB. Eligible queued events are kept for at most seven days and cleaned up on the next access. Closed apps run no dedicated deletion process for this. Limits of 60 upload attempts and 512 KiB of payload per month are provided; network and encryption overhead is additional.

Server-side analytics cleanup normally runs every 15 minutes. It removes raw events, batches and coin snapshots once they were received 89 days and 23 hours ago, allowing a safety margin within the normal 90-day window. Expired active associations and their dependent data are also removed. Failures or a paused service can delay processing; physical deletion at an exact time is not guaranteed.

Limited consent, registration and replay-security evidence may normally remain independently of gameplay events for up to 90 days after creation of the respective record. Confirmed deletion status is retained for seven days. These records contain no messages, screenshots or complete saves. Provider logs and possible safety copies follow the separate rules in sections 8 and 9.

Withdrawal and server deletion

You can withdraw optional analysis through Settings → “Ads and consent settings” or the privacy options offered there. New collection and uploads stop, and unsent optional events are discarded. Protected deletion authorization is retained solely for the outstanding deletion check. Offline requests are kept locally and processed only during later active online use.

When an authorized withdrawal reaches the server, it blocks the installation and attempts to erase the active association and its events, batches, balance snapshots and participation records. Failed deletion remains pending for retry. An offline request, successful transmission attempt or local withdrawal display is not confirmed server deletion. No general 24-hour guarantee is given. Analytics withdrawal does not erase saves, purchases or support conversations.

15. Help, support messages and images

You can contact us by email. Where in-app support is enabled for you, you can deliberately send a request with its category, message and language, and optionally a screenshot you select. Developer replies, your further replies and read status belong to the same conversation. Your own messages do not trigger a new developer-reply notice, and opening the main menu alone does not mark replies as read.

Support is independent of optional gameplay statistics. The app does not automatically attach saves, coin balances, purchase receipts or consent signals. Check text and images before sending and do not include passwords, purchase tokens, payment information or sensitive data. Technical image checks and re-encoding do not anonymise visible image content.

The Supabase service privately processes the ticket identifier, messages, image, replies, status, timestamps and read markers to handle the request. A random secret ticket credential protects access. Credentials and confirmed local text copies are stored separately from saves in device-bound Android Keystore/AES-GCM storage. A cached conversation can remain readable offline; an attachment notice does not necessarily mean the image itself is cached. The developer can read messages and images through the protected support inbox. There is no public image URL or end-to-end encryption against support staff.

Reply status is checked sparingly only during active foreground use, without a permanent background connection or additional Android push permission. Pending read confirmation may be retained locally and securely synchronized later. A network failure is not automatically treated as confirmed submission, reading or deletion.

Support tickets become due for server-side deletion 90 days after the last message or status change, and no later than 180 days after ticket creation. Viewing alone does not extend this period. Automatic cleanup normally runs every 15 minutes and removes due tickets together with messages, any image and read status. A content-free hashed deletion marker may remain until its cleanup after seven days to prevent retries. Failures or service pauses can delay processing; physical deletion at an exact time is not guaranteed. Independently of this cleanup, public in-app support submission remains blocked pending separate approval; email contact remains available.

Where ticket access is enabled, you can use “Delete request”. After successful server processing, the ticket, conversation, optional image and read status are removed. Local copies and outstanding deletion operations are separate; automatic time-based deletion of the local conversation cache is not implemented. Without connectivity, deletion remains pending. Anonymous access cannot be guaranteed after data loss or uninstallation without the ticket credential. General contact and rights routes remain available.

Deliberately requested support services rely on Article 6(1)(b) GDPR; necessary abuse prevention relies on Article 6(1)(f), with the affected interests balanced. Email correspondence is retained as needed to handle the request and any applicable legal obligations; the intended ticket periods are not an automatic email-mailbox deletion policy.

16. Google Play Integrity and app attestation

Enabled registration or ticket creation may use Google Play Integrity for abuse prevention. Checks cover the app package, signature, version, Play licensing or device integrity and a short-lived request binding. The integrity token is sent to the server over encrypted transport and decoded with Google only there; access keys remain server-side. Application code does not persist raw tokens in logs or the database.

Valid app attestation proves neither a player's age nor analytics consent. Support requests do not require gameplay consent. Optional analysis still requires separate individual eligibility and specific consent. Play Store age or parental-approval information is not an analytics field either. Necessary app-abuse prevention relies on Article 6(1)(f) GDPR with the interests balanced; optional gameplay analysis itself remains consent-based.
